Backed by research

Study techniques worth your time

Ranked by utility, based on the cognitive-science literature on learning and memory (Dunlosky et al., 2013, and related retrieval-practice research). Open a card to see why it works and how to use it during a study period.

Worth knowing: the study habits that feel most productive — rereading notes and highlighting a textbook — are consistently rated low utility in the research. They create familiarity, not retrieval strength. Swap time spent rereading for time spent self-testing.
